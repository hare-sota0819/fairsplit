# FairSplit Brand — reset

**2026-08-19: all previous design and branding decisions are retired.** The
owner is doing a full design overhaul; nothing from the earlier brand books
(v1 2026-08-14, v2 2026-08-18: palette, typeface, chrome grammar, navigation
motion, Sem's visual form, chat surface bans, reference inventory) carries
forward. Do not cite or reuse them.

The ONE decision that survives:

## The assistant's name

- The in-chat settlement assistant is **셈** (English: **Sem**) — native
  Korean for counting/reckoning (셈하다, 셈이 빠르다).
- The app name stays **FairSplit**.

Everything else — visual identity, voice details, motion, layout — is open
and will be defined by the new design brief.
